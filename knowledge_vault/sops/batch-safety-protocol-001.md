Batch safety verification requires three checks: visual inspection of the seal, confirmation of the lot code against the release record, and a pressure test at the rated limit.

A batch that fails any check must be quarantined and reported to the quality team. Quarantined batches are not released until a reviewer signs off.

Triage status for a batch is one of released, quarantined, or pending review.
